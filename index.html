About Me

Hi, I'm Molly!

I'm an easy-going, enthusiastic English tutor with 4+ years of teaching experience across the UK, Spain and Mexico. I'm passionate about English and love helping students build confidence, improve their skills, and develop a genuine enjoyment of the subject.

I'm experienced in all areas of English, having taught IB and A-Level English Literature, GCSE English Literature and Language, as well as English as a foreign language. I've taught both online and in the classroom, and have experience supporting students from a wide range of backgrounds and abilities.

I offer personalised 1-to-1 English lessons designed around your goals.

Whether you're learning English for school, university, work, travel or everyday communication, lessons are adapted to your individual level, interests and learning goals.

Teaching Philosophy

Tuently is an online tutoring platform with the primary aim of building confidence in students and helping them to achieve their target grades. I aim to show students their true ability by teaching them in a way that suits their individual learning style.
